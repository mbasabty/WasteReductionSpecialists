Current Problem


The current situation is that in urban areas, between 70 and slightly over 90 percent of households had waste collection services. The informal settlements were a challenge. In the primarily rural provinces, the percentage of waste collected fell below 60 percent. Due to municipalities' limited impact, waste collection in standard regions was somewhat less than 40%. Over the years, the nation's average access to waste collection services has stayed relatively stable at little over 70%. Waste disposal was favored above other solutions due to widespread underpricing of waste management, which meant that consumers and business did not fully understand the costs of garbage management. (https://pmg.org.za/committee-meeting/34368/)


National Waste Management Strategy

The National Waste Management Strategy (NWMS) was created in accordance with Section 6 of the National Environmental Management : Waste Act (NEMWA), Ms. Musekene informed the Committee. Over the course of five years, the NWMS was put into operation. The DFFE was putting this third NWMS into practice.
