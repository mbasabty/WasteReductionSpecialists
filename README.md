For my final Practical Assessment Task for Grade 12 Computer Applications Technology, I was a Grade 12 learner at Queen’s College Boys’ 
High School in the Eastern Cape. I investigated jobs in the Waste and Management sector to raise awareness of environmental 
sustainability, focusing on Waste Reduction Specialists and their role in improving waste treatment through material recovery facilities 
and organic waste diversion instead of landfilling.In South Africa, access to waste collection varied, with over 70% in urban areas, 
but lower rates in rural and some regions, highlighting ongoing challenges in waste management. This showed the need for more 
sustainable solutions. I distributed questionnaires to public and private waste management companies and observed their operations. 
I used Microsoft Access, Excel, and HTML to analyse the data and present my findings in a website and Word document. The target audience 
included stakeholders in environmental management in the Chris Hani District Municipality, especially Queenstown.
