What Does a Waste Management Specialist Do?

A waste management specialist plans and coordinates waste reclamation and reduction projects, such as recycling programs or waste collection services. As a waste management specialist, your responsibilities include identifying and quantifying the scope of projects, developing budgets, researching contractual and budgetary obligations, and working with local stakeholders to ensure that the project remains in compliance with local or state waste management regulation. You work closely with other waste management specialists and government officials.

How to Become a Waste Management Specialist

To become a waste management specialist, you need a bachelor’s degree in laboratory science or a related field and at least a year of experience in the waste management industry. Study local and state regulations, laws, and policies regarding environmental management and waste services, as well as a thorough understanding of the specific types of services you may oversee, such as water disposal or recycling. Intern or obtain an entry-level position with a private waste management firm or a government agency that handles waste disposal to gain experience in the industry. Essential skills for the job include verbal and written communication and analytical thinking.
